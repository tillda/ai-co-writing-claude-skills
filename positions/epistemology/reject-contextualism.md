# Reject Contextualism

DeRose/Lewis contextualism is implausible: "know" does not behave like "here" or "tall". The motivating cases are better explained by shifts in psychological confidence and conversational salience, not in semantic content.

**Applicable to:** contextualism, knowledge ascriptions, conversational standards, DeRose, Lewis Elusive Knowledge, shifting standards, *epistemology / 9. Other Replies to Scepticism: Moore, Putnam, Contextualism*
