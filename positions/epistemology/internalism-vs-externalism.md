# Internalism vs Externalism: Both, in Different Senses

Both contain something right. Distinguish *animal* knowledge (broadly externalist — reliable processes suffice) from *reflective* knowledge (internalist — requires reflectively accessible justifiers). Pure reliabilism fails the new evil demon and generality problems; pure access internalism is too demanding for ordinary perceptual knowledge.
