# Argument from Illusion / Hallucination Fails

Illusion shows only that things sometimes appear other than they are, not that we perceive mental intermediaries. Hallucination shows there is a common phenomenal element across veridical and non-veridical experience (against disjunctivism), but the common element is the *experience*, not a sense-datum we are aware of.

**Applicable to:** argument from illusion, argument from hallucination, common-element thesis, indistinguishability, perceptual experience and hallucination, *epistemology / 5. Perception*
