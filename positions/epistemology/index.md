# Philosophical Positions — Epistemology (PY1025)

These are my stated leanings on questions in this course. They follow Michael Huemer's *Understanding Knowledge* (UK) chapter by chapter. When the prompt is silent on direction, default to these positions as the **conclusion** of the essay. Use the theory-rejection structure honestly: present rival theories in their strongest form, raise the genuine objections to them, then arrive at the position below as the considered view. Do not strawman the rejected positions — the examiner can tell.

When the prompt directs a specific position, follow the prompt; these defaults apply only when the prompt is silent.
