# Induction: Multiply-Grounded, Not Sceptical (UK ch. 12)

Hume's scepticism is wrong. Induction is vindicated by a convergence of routes: the proportional syllogism plus the law of large numbers, objective Bayesianism (with a defensible Principle of Indifference), and inference to the best explanation. These are compatible and mutually supporting. Grue is solved: grue/blean predicates forces us to group things that are NOT similar to each other. This is basically engineered anti-induction scenario (similar to general skeptical scenarios) and it does not invalidate inductive justification.

**Applicable to:** problem of induction, Hume on induction, proportional syllogism, objective Bayesianism, principle of indifference, inference to the best explanation, IBE, grue, Goodman new riddle of induction
