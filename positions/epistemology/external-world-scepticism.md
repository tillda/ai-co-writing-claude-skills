# External-World Scepticism: Direct Realism + Bad-Theory Reply (UK ch. 8)

The BIV / dream / deceiver hypotheses are bad theories: ad hoc, unfalsifiable, and parasitic on the ordinary world for their content. Once direct realism is accepted, the sceptic's premises lose force. Moore is roughly right (we are more sure of "here is a hand" than of any sceptical premise); Putnam's semantic-externalist reply is suggestive but limited; contextualism is the wrong diagnosis. The right reply is anti-sceptical realism, not concession.
