# Religious Knowledge: Evidentialism (UK ch. 16)

Evidentialism is correct: religious belief should be proportioned to evidence. Reject fideism. Religious testimony is weak (traditions conflict, no independently verifiable distinctive feats). Religious experience gives PC-style prima facie justification but is heavily defeated by cultural variability and conflict. Philosophical arguments (fine-tuning vs. problem of evil) are where the real action is.

**Applicable to:** religious knowledge, evidentialism, fideism, faith vs evidence, religious experience, sensus divinitatis, Plantinga reformed epistemology, fine-tuning, problem of evil
