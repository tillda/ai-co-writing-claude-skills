# Against Knowledge as Primitive

The right diagnosis is Wittgensteinian, not Williamsonian. Knowledge resists analysis because of how concepts work in general (family resemblance), not because knowledge is a sui generis mental state with no decomposition. Reject Williamson's knowledge-first programme.

**Applicable to:** knowledge first, primitivism about knowledge, Williamson, knowledge as a mental state, Craig, *epistemology / 2. Truth-Tracking, Belief, and Knowledge as Primitive*
