## What to look for

- **Large or bold text** that stands out from regular body text
- **Chapter numbers** with or without titles (e.g., "3", "Chapter 3", "III", "CHAPTER THREE")
- **Section titles** in larger or different font than body text
- Pages that appear to start a new section (often with more whitespace at top, content starting lower on the page)
- Copyright pages: look for small text with copyright symbol, year, publisher, ISBN — even without a heading

## What to IGNORE

- Running headers (small repeated text at the very top of pages, like the book title or author name repeated across many pages)
- Page numbers (printed inside the book)
- Regular body text paragraphs
- Section subheadings within a chapter (smaller headings that subdivide chapter content)
- Footnotes or endnote numbers

## Important

- Only report pages where a MAJOR new section begins
- Most pages will NOT have a chapter start — that is normal
- If none of the pages in this batch start a new section, return an empty array
- If a page is a start of new first-level chapter, there usually is NONE normal text above this chapter heading. (Chapter Foo might have by coincidence second-level subchapter "Chapter Bar" which might also exist as main chapter)
- If source is a book it ALMOST ALWAYS has one or two table of content!
