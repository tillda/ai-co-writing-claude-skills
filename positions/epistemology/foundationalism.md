# Foundationalism (UK ch. 4)

Foundationalism is correct. Coherentism fails the alternate-coherent-systems / fairy-tale objection and ultimately needs foundations anyway. Infinitism fails the finite-mind objection. Global scepticism is self-defeating. Some beliefs must be non-inferentially justified.
