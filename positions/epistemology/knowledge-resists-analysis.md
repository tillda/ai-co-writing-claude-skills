# Knowledge Resists Analysis (UK ch. 2)

JTB is refuted by Gettier; every patch (no-false-lemmas, reliabilism, proper function, sensitivity, safety, relevant alternatives, defeasibility) fails to a counterexample. No analysis works because "knowledge" is a Wittgensteinian / family-resemblance concept, not a Lockean one with necessary-and-sufficient conditions.

**Applicable to:** justified true belief, JTB, Gettier, Gettier cases, knowledge as JTB, sufficiency of the tripartite analysis, no-false-lemmas, defeasibility, conceptual analysis of knowledge, *epistemology / 1. The Tripartite Analysis and Gettier Cases*
