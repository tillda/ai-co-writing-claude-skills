# Peer Disagreement: Total Evidence View (UK ch. 19)

Reject pure equal-weight (it self-defeats and ignores first-order evidence) and pure steadfastness (it licenses dogmatism). On the Total Evidence View, first-order evidence can downgrade an apparent peer's reliability — if your interlocutor asserts something prima facie absurd, that is itself evidence they are not in fact your peer on this question. Most philosophical disagreement reflects gestalt-perception differences rather than crisp factual disputes.

**Applicable to:** peer disagreement, equal weight view, steadfast view, right reasons view, total evidence view, conciliationism, gestalt perception in philosophy
