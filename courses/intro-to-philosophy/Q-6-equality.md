# Williams on need vs merit and the irrationality of wealth-based distribution

**Question(s)**:
    According to Williams, how does the distinction between need and merit bear on questions about equality? Is Williams right about this?
    According to Williams, a system that distributes certain goods, such as medical services or rights before the law, on the basis of actual inequalities in wealth or power is inherently irrational. How does Williams argue for this claim? Are his arguments convincing?

# Williams vs Nozick — medical care and barbering

**Question(s)**:
    According to Williams, the only proper criterion for the distribution of medical care is medical need. According to Nozick, this would lead to the absurd conclusion that the only proper criterion for the distribution of barbering services is barbering need. Expound Williams's and Nozick's arguments and assess their strengths.

# Williams on equality of opportunity vs equality of respect

**Question(s)**:
    Williams distinguishes between two elements in the idea of equality: equality of opportunity and equality of respect. What is the difference between these? Is there any reason to think that there could be a problem in practice of combining equality of opportunity with equality of respect?

# Williams on equality of respect and hierarchy

**Question(s)**:
    "Once, however, one accepts [...] that the degree of man's consciousness about [...] his role in society is itself in some part the product of social arrangements, and that it can be increased, the idea of a stable hierarchy must, I think, disappear." Explain and assess the argument that Williams constructs on this basis against the suggestion that equality of respect might be compatible with the existence of a hierarchical society.
