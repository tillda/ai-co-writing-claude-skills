# Phenomenal Conservatism (UK ch. 5)

The ground of foundational justification is appearance: if it seems to S that p (and S has no defeater), then S has prima facie justification to believe p. PC handles perception, memory, intuition, and testimony uniformly. The acquaintance theory founders on the Sellarsian dilemma; qualified-appearance theories add nothing. PC is not self-defeating.

**Applicable to:** phenomenal conservatism, seemings, appearances, foundational justification, prima facie justification, acquaintance theory, Sellarsian dilemma, BonJour, Fumerton, Pryor dogmatism, easy knowledge, self-defeat objection to PC
