# The 'little finger' implication of Locke's view

**Question(s)**:
    At one point in his discussion of personal identity, Locke suggests that on his view, if the whole of a person's consciousness were to somehow be contained in their little finger, then 'it is evident the little finger would be the person'. Explain the grounds on which Locke makes this claim. Do you find this implication of his view to be persuasive?
